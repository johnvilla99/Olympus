# templates/decisions/

Templates for decision records.

## Purpose

Ensure decisions capture context, options, rationale, and consequences — not just the outcome.

## What belongs here

- Decision record templates (ADR, PDR, GDR, and ecosystem-level decisions)

## What does not belong here

- Exploratory concept records (use `templates/knowledge-assets/` or `docs/concepts/`)
- Governance standards (use `templates/governance/`)
- Filed decision records (use `docs/decisions/`)

## Primary template

- `DECISION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md`
