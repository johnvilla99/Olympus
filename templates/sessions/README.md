# templates/sessions/

Templates for AI and human session continuity.

## Purpose

Reduce context loss between sessions by standardizing how work starts and hands off.

## What belongs here

- Session start and handoff templates
- Structured continuity artifacts meant for copy/paste or light filing

## What does not belong here

- Durable Knowledge Assets that should live in `docs/` (promote when appropriate)
- Raw daily logs (use `logs/daily/`)
- Application code or automation

## Templates

- `SESSION_START_TEMPLATE.md` — begin a session with required reading and constraints
- `SESSION_HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md` — end a session with state, decisions, and next steps

Filled copies for Olympus work belong in `logs/daily/`. Evidence packets are a section within handoffs (no standalone template). See OLY-GOV-004.
