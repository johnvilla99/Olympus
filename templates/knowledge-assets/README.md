# templates/knowledge-assets/

Templates for general Knowledge Assets.

## Purpose

Standardize metadata and section structure for durable knowledge containers across all asset types.

## What belongs here

- Knowledge Asset templates and variants

## What does not belong here

- Completed assets (use `docs/`)
- Decision-specific templates (use `templates/decisions/`)
- Session handoff templates (use `templates/sessions/`)

## Primary template

- `KNOWLEDGE_ASSET_TEMPLATE.md`
