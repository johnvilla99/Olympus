# templates/governance/

Templates for governance standards and process definitions.

## Purpose

Provide a consistent structure for standards that define how work is done in the ecosystem.

## What belongs here

- Governance standard templates
- Process and documentation standard templates

## What does not belong here

- Individual decision records (use `templates/decisions/`)
- Exploratory governance ideas without standard structure
- Approved standards (file completed copies in `docs/governance/`)

## Primary template

- `GOVERNANCE_STANDARD_TEMPLATE.md`
