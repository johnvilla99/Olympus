## Decision

1. **Olympus** is the official name of the ecosystem.
2. **Olympus and Hobby Brain are separate projects.** Neither is a subproject, implementation branch, or subordinate initiative of the other.
3. Hobby Brain's historical role in inspiring Olympus does not create governance, repository, product, or implementation authority over Olympus.
4. Olympus does not automatically govern Hobby Brain.
5. Any future relationship in which Hobby Brain adopts, participates in, consumes, or contributes to Olympus governance or capabilities requires a separate explicit decision.

## Context

OLY-DB-001 asked whether Mt. Olympus was the official ecosystem name. OLY-DB-007 asked which project would participate first and assumed Hobby Brain might occupy that role because Olympus originated from thinking developed during Hobby Brain work.

John clarified that Olympus is now its own project and ecosystem, while Hobby Brain remains its own project. The historical origin is relevant context but does not establish an ongoing structural relationship.

## Rationale

This decision prevents origin history from becoming accidental authority. It gives Olympus a stable identity while preserving Hobby Brain's independence.

The separation allows each project to establish its own product, architecture, repository, agents, decisions, and operating practices. A future relationship may still be useful, but it must be deliberately defined rather than inferred.

## Consequences

- Olympus documentation must use **Olympus** as the official ecosystem name.
- References to **Mt. Olympus** may remain in historical titles or records but should not imply a separate ecosystem.
- Olympus and Hobby Brain must maintain separate project authority, repositories, product definitions, architecture decisions, and continuity records.
- Hobby Brain is not automatically a participating Olympus project.
- Future participation or adoption requires an explicit governance decision defining scope, authority, and boundaries.
- OLY-DB-001 and OLY-DB-007 are resolved.

## Explicit Non-Decisions

This decision does not determine:

- whether Hobby Brain will later participate in Olympus;
- how participating projects are admitted or governed;
- what every participating repository must expose;
- how Olympus software will integrate with external projects;
- product or architecture design for the Olympus application.
