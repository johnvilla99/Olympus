# docs/lessons/

Lessons learned from success, failure, surprise, and experience.

## Purpose

Convert experience into durable knowledge so future work avoids repeated rediscovery.

## What belongs here

- Postmortems and retrospectives promoted to assets
- Engineering, product, and governance lessons with context
- What worked, what failed, and what would be done differently

## What does not belong here

- Raw incident tickets or unprocessed notes
- Binding standards (use `docs/governance/`)
- Decisions that need a formal decision record (use `docs/decisions/`)
- Project-specific secrets or credentials
